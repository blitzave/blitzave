Clarity over money.

BlitZave is a free personal finance app that lives on your own laptop. It reads your accounts, sorts your spending, and shows you what you're actually doing with money. Calmly, and without keeping score.

Lives on your laptop

Everything stays in one SQLite file in your home folder. Copy it, back it up, take it with you.

Read-only, always

Your bank connects through your own SimpleFIN account. It can see; it can never move money.

Nothing phones home

The app answers only on 127.0.0.1. The one network call is your bank sync. No analytics, ever.

Free and open

Apache-2.0, built in the open. BlitZave itself costs nothing, today and for good.

An ally, never a scorekeeper

Most money apps keep score. BlitZave has your back.

It reports what happened and leaves the judgment out. No red numbers for spending, no streaks to break, no guilt.

Transactions

One calm month at a time

Every account, every transaction, a month per page. Search, filter, and re-sync any single month from your bank with one click.

  • Checking, savings and credit cards together
  • Splits and tags when one category isn't enough

Rules

Teach it once

Categorize a purchase and choose "all transactions like this." BlitZave matches the merchant through store numbers, wallet prefixes and small typos, and every future sync follows.

  • Your own corrections always win
  • The bank's record is never rewritten

Budgets

Context, not alarms

Monthly budgets carry forward. A marker shows how much of the month has passed, and the pace is told in plain words. Going over is information, not a failure state.

Adjustments

Every dollar counted once

Link a card payment or a transfer between your accounts so it never counts as spending, and link a refund to the charge it reverses. Every balance still reconciles.

Get started

Running in a few minutes.

One command to start. One token to paste. That's the whole setup.

  1. 1

    Install uv

    uv runs Python projects, and installs Python 3.12 for you if you need it.

    curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh
  2. 2

    Install and start

    One install gives you a blitzave command. The first run creates your data folder at ~/BlitZave.

    uv tool install git+https://github.com/blitzave/blitzave
blitzave
  3. 3

    Connect, once

    BlitZave opens 127.0.0.1:8742 in your browser. Paste a setup token from SimpleFIN Bridge. Your accounts arrive with the last 90 days.

    # then, any day
blitzave

You'll need a SimpleFIN Bridge account (a small subscription, paid to SimpleFIN) and a Mac. Linux should work too.
